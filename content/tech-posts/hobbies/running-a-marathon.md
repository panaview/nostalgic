I started running a year ago, with the main goal to stay in shape. At first, it only started
with relatively reasonable goals on the short term (run a 10k event under an hour, etc), but it quickly evolved (degenerated ?)
into training for multiple 10k races, a half-marathon, and finally, completing an entire marathon.

Even though I was already in decent shape at the time I got started, I had to deal with health challenges
caused by my lack of mobility and bad day-to-day posture. Running more than a few kilometers caused knee pain
to flare up frequently, and I had to stop training for periods of time due to frequent runner's knee occurences.

With time, proper medical follow-ups, and stretching (lots of stretching!), I stopped having
knee pain and could cover larger distances at a consistent pace.

During the first few months, I wasn't that much of a consistent runner: I'd go out for 1-2 runs on a weekly basis.
After completing my first 10k events, I started picking-up the pace ahead of the half-marathon and marathon.

At the peak of training, I was running 4 times a week with mileage up-to 55 km a week.
